Step 1

Submit the KN.1 Form (คง.1) for Laboratory Access

Request the use of equipment and laboratories at the Center for Scientific and Technological Equipment. You need the KN.1 form printed from the system and your Safety Card (safety training certificate).

  1. Fill in the form in the KN.1 system

    Go to cste.sut.ac.th/Project1, complete all fields, then export the file to PDF.

  2. Get your advisor’s signature

    Send the KN.1 form to your project advisor. An electronic signature is accepted.

  3. Send the signed form (PDF) to the School Secretary

    Via LINE or e-mail: wannaporn@sut.ac.th

  4. The Secretary forwards it to the Head of School

    Please wait for the document.

  5. The Secretary forwards it to the Dean

    This takes 1–2 working days. Once approved, the form is returned to you.

  6. Upload the fully signed form to the system

    Receive the file from the School, then upload it to cste.sut.ac.th/Project1.

Problems with the KN.1 system? Contact Ms. Nongnapas (P’Koi), tel. 0-4422-3215.

Step 2

Apply for Project Funding

Apply through the Undergraduate Project Management System. Funding is paid in 2 installments, and both must be submitted within the trimester in which you are enrolled in the project course.

Open from week 2 of the trimester

Installment 1

  • Signatures of all group members, uploaded in the Undergraduate Project Management System
  • Project proposal on form FM-ENG-PROJECT-PROPOSAL, reviewed by your advisor
  • Copies of the bank book of every member, combined into one PDF file
  • Course registration records (Reg) of every member, combined into one PDF file
Submit after receiving installment 1

Installment 2

  • Progress report on form FM-ENG-PROJECT-PROGRESS, reviewed by your advisor
  • Proof of payment (receipts)
  • Spending plan for installment 2 (see example)

When to submit installment 2: you have all receipts for installment 1 but spent more than its amount, or you have only some of the receipts for installment 1 so far.
